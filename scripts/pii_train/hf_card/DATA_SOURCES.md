# Training data of akana-pii-tagger

Both models were fine-tuned for token classification (BIO tags of nine labels) from dbmdz's Turkish
models: `dbmdz/electra-small-turkish-cased-discriminator` (the fast model) and
`dbmdz/bert-base-turkish-cased` (BERTurk, averaged with the small model in akana's accurate mode),
both MIT. The training mix is listed below with each source's licence. "Teacher labels" are labels
written by Claude Sonnet following akana's annotation guideline
(`scripts/pii_data/annotation_guideline.md` in the akana repository); every value in them is a span
of the source text, nothing was added to the texts.

Because the weights learnt from text under CC BY-SA 4.0 (Wikipedia, WikiNER, MusteriYorumlari),
they are released under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Labelled and generated data

| Part | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Synthetic scenarios (28,000 texts) | akana's own templates (`scripts/pii_data/templates.py`), filled with generated values | own work |
| BTX24 (40,000 texts) | [BTX24/turkish-privacy-pii-ner](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BTX24/turkish-privacy-pii-ner), train split, values refilled with generated ones | CC BY 4.0 |
| Belgin | [negentropi/belgin-pii-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/negentropi/belgin-pii-dataset), train split, values refilled | CC BY 4.0 |
| WikiNER persons | [turkish-nlp-suite/turkish-wikiNER](https://huggingface.co/datasets/turkish-nlp-suite/turkish-wikiNER) (Altinok, 2023), train split | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Hard cases | technical and form texts generated by Claude Sonnet, kept where two labellings agree | own work |

## Real text with teacher labels

| Domain | Units | Source | Licence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Court decisions | 4,088 | Türk İçtihat Korpusu: decisions of Yargıtay, Danıştay, UYAP emsal and the Constitutional Court | CC0 1.0; court decisions carry no copyright (FSEK art. 31) |
| News | 249 | [eneSadi/turkuaz-rag](https://huggingface.co/datasets/eneSadi/turkuaz-rag) | MIT |
| Tweets | 2,524 | [yankihue/tweets-turkish](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yankihue/tweets-turkish) | none declared |
| Voice-assistant requests | 1,967 | MASSIVE, Turkish ([mteb/amazon_massive_intent](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mteb/amazon_massive_intent); FitzGerald et al., 2022) | CC BY 4.0 (MASSIVE); Apache-2.0 (the mteb copy) |
| Prompts written to chatbots | 720 | [beratcmn/turkish-prompt-injections](https://huggingface.co/datasets/beratcmn/turkish-prompt-injections) (Apache-2.0), [fevziegeyurtsevenler/turkish-prompt-injection](https://huggingface.co/datasets/fevziegeyurtsevenler/turkish-prompt-injection) (CC BY 4.0), [AltaySec/turkish-llm-injection](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AltaySec/turkish-llm-injection) (CC BY 4.0), [fevziegeyurtsevenler/turkish-over-refusal-set](https://huggingface.co/datasets/fevziegeyurtsevenler/turkish-over-refusal-set) (Apache-2.0), [fevziegeyurtsevenler/guardrail-arena](https://huggingface.co/datasets/fevziegeyurtsevenler/guardrail-arena) (Apache-2.0) | as listed |
| Social-media comments | 1,304 | [Overfit-GM/turkish-toxic-language](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Overfit-GM/turkish-toxic-language) | Apache-2.0 |
| Wikipedia | 2,317 | Turkish Wikipedia, 217 articles; `wikipedia_attribution.tsv` lists each article with the revision the text was taken from | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Maths word problems | 848 | [GSM8K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openai/gsm8k) (Cobbe et al., 2021), 808 questions of its train split, translated into Turkish for this model by Claude Sonnet | MIT (GSM8K) |
| Product reviews | 1,048 | [turkish-nlp-suite/MusteriYorumlari](https://huggingface.co/datasets/turkish-nlp-suite/MusteriYorumlari) (Altinok), train split; reviews its curators collected from Hepsiburada and Trendyol: 920 units sampled at random, plus the 128 further units that mention a calendar date (order and delivery dates are personal data) | CC BY-SA 4.0 (as its curators declare) |

A unit is a paragraph or a run of sentences of at most ~400 characters. Each unit was seen twice per
epoch. A share of all training texts was also added in lower case, in capitals or without Turkish
letters.
